SatLab

Interactive simulators for learning how satellite communications work. Work through them in order, because each lab builds on the one before.

Learning path

1. Peaker

Point a ground antenna at a real satellite, find its beacon on a spectrum analyzer, and step through how auto-peak and step-track work.

Start here.

2. Mixer Lab

Follow signals through a bench mixer and a full satellite link. See images, spurs, compression and third-order products.

Builds on: reading a spectrum analyzer.

3. Interference Lab

See how interference affects a satellite link, then diagnose trouble tickets the way an operator would.

Builds on: beacons from Peaker, compression and the TWTA from Mixer Lab.

4. DVB-S2 Overlay Lab

Follow video through a full broadcast chain, then watch a stronger signal break and capture the receiver, and how operators detect it.

Builds on: C/(N+I), margin and IQ plots from Interference Lab.
